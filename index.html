Flagship product

PromptNext Prompt governance for enterprise teams

The problem

Operations teams were writing ad-hoc prompts with no quality control, no reuse and no way to prove value.

What I did

Sized the opportunity, wrote the business case and won funding on the strength of the proof of concept, approved by Infosys BPM Technology Solutions Group leadership. Then I built it end to end, with Claude as my build partner, and I own it today.

The product

Every prompt moves from Draft to Reviewed to Certified, and only certified prompts enter the shared library. Users rate prompts, every use is tracked, and a dashboard shows hours saved, prompt reuse and how each team is doing.

Where it stands

  • In use 300+ users across 7 teams in its first month after launch.
  • Next Approved for rollout across the Financial Services unit, subject to client permission.
