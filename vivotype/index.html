Dictation for Mac · v0.3.0

Let go of the key, and the text is already there.

Hold a key, talk, let go. VivoType turns your speech into text on your own Mac and types it into whatever app you're in. It's near-instant: a sentence usually appears in under a second on my Mac, because nothing goes to a server. No cloud, no account, no upload of your voice.

Near-instant: the model stays loaded, so there's no wait Speech recognition runs on your Mac No network calls while you dictate MIT licensed → GitHub

Made for Indian English

"Rs 500" or "500 rupees"→"₹500"
"100k users"→"1 lakh users"
"Bangalore"→"Bengaluru"

A clean-up step on your Mac fixes rupee amounts, common Indian place names and the spelling of names in your contacts. You can add your own words, and turn the rupee change off in apps where you don't want it, like a code editor.

Get it

v0.3.0. Install with Homebrew, no coding needed.

brew tap kalpitt/vivotype brew install --cask vivotype

No Homebrew? Use the one-line installer or the download in the install guide.

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) on macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs aren't supported. The first launch downloads the speech model (about 466 MB) once.

What it doesn't do

  • English only. VivoType does not understand Hindi or other languages yet.
  • Mac only. The engine doesn't depend on macOS, but there is no Windows app.
  • Dictation, not meeting notes. It types what you say. It doesn't record or transcribe meetings.
