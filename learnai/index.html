learnai.how: practical AI for your day job

Workflows and prompt tools for Indian working professionals: the weekly status report, the escalation note, the meeting prep, the appraisal. Each page gives you something to run today in the AI tool you already use. No AI news.

How it works

  • Bring your own AI. Each tool builds a prompt from your answers, and you run it in your own ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. The site has no server of its own, so nothing you work on is uploaded.
  • Start by role. Guides for managers, analysts, engineers, HR and operations people point to the workflows that fit their week.
  • Written for Indian offices. Appraisal cycles, status updates, and advice that starts with free plans before paid ones.
